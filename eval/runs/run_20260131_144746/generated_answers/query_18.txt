## Summary

Complex queries are processed by first attempting to retrieve an optimized execution plan from a `QueryPlanner`'s `plan_cache`. If a plan is not cached, the `QueryPlanner` generates a new plan by applying various optimizer rules. These rules include `_apply_constant_folding` for simplifying expressions, `_estimate_cost` and `_estimate_selectivity` (which leverage `can_use_index`) to evaluate the efficiency of different operations, and the overall `_generate_plan_steps` logic to determine the optimal sequence of `PlanOperation`s. Once generated, the optimized plan is stored in the cache for future reuse. The `ExecutionEngine` then takes this plan and executes each `PlanStep` sequentially, recording detailed execution timing and statistics (like `start_time`, `duration_ms`, `rows_processed`) within an `ExecutionContext` for the query, and aggregating these into overall `execution_stats`. This combination ensures that queries are executed efficiently, leveraging pre-computed plans when possible and optimizing new ones, while providing comprehensive performance metrics.
