## Summary

For complex queries, the system employs a sophisticated process involving plan caching, sequential application of five distinct optimizer rules, and detailed timing measurements. The `QueryPlanner` acts as the orchestrator, first checking its cache for an existing, optimized plan. A cache hit provides immediate access to a pre-optimized plan, bypassing further processing. In case of a cache miss, the `QueryPlanner` generates an initial plan, which is then passed to the `QueryOptimizer`. The `QueryOptimizer` applies Predicate Pushdown, Projection Pushdown, Constant Folding, Index Selection, and Join Reordering rules in sequence, measuring the time taken for this optimization. The resulting optimized plan is then cached by the `QueryPlanner` for future reuse. Finally, the `ExecutionEngine` takes the (cached or newly optimized) plan and executes its steps, also tracking the total execution duration. This combined approach aims to minimize both planning and execution time, especially for frequently run or computationally intensive complex queries.
