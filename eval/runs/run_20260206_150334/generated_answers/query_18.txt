## Summary

The system processes complex queries by integrating optimizer rules, plan caching, and execution timing. Optimizer rules, such as `CONSTANT_FOLDING` and `PREDICATE_PUSHDOWN` (with others listed as potential rules), are intended to transform a query into a more efficient execution plan. This optimized `QueryPlan` is then managed by the `QueryPlanner`. It creates an `ExecutionContext` to meticulously track the query's performance, recording the `start_time`, `end_time`, and the `duration_ms` for the entire query, as well as individual `step_duration` for each operation within the plan. This detailed timing information, along with `rows_processed`, is aggregated into `execution_stats` and `execution_stages`, providing comprehensive insights into the query's runtime behavior and the effectiveness of the optimization and caching strategies.
