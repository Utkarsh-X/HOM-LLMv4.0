For complex queries, the system employs a sophisticated pipeline involving plan caching, a five-rule optimizer, and a detailed execution engine with timing capabilities. This integrated approach ensures that complex queries are processed efficiently, leveraging past computations through caching, improving execution paths through optimization, and providing comprehensive performance monitoring.
